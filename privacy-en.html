Same Moon

Privacy Policy

Effective: November 4, 2026

Same Moon (おなじ月, "the app") is an app for sharing the same moment with strangers by looking up at the moon. The app keeps only what it needs to find where people's moments overlap. It does not collect real names, photos, or precise locations.

This policy explains what the app receives, what it is used for, and when it is deleted.

This English version is a translation of the Japanese policy. If the two differ, the Japanese version prevails.

Masahiko Suzuki (individual)

What we do not collect

  • Real names, photos, phone numbers, or addresses
  • Precise location (latitude and longitude)
  • Contacts, or information about other apps
  • Advertising identifiers

The app shows no ads, and uses no analytics tools to study how it is used.

What stays on your device

The following is never sent to the server.

  • Location: To work out when the moon rises and where it is in the sky, the app receives your approximate location once, and replaces it with the nearest city. The location itself is kept neither on your device nor on the server. You can use the app without allowing location access (you can choose a city yourself).
  • Device orientation: Used to check that you are looking up toward the moon. Not kept.
  • Notification settings and notifications: Notifications for moonrise and named nights are timed on your device.
  • Calendar records: The dates of the nights you looked up, and how many seconds you looked up. At the turn of the year, they are folded into one sheet for the year.
  • Words you receive: Words you receive stay only on your device.
  • Saving to Photos: The app writes to Photos only when you save the sheet for a year as an image. It never reads your photos.

What other people can see

  • Your kindred: Your nickname, a place no finer than a city ("Around Sendai"), and the dates and times of the nights you overlapped.
  • People who looked up at the same time: The number of people, and the city of the farthest person (not who they are).
  • The person who receives your words: The text, and the date and moon name of the night it was left. They cannot tell who left it.

Service providers

The server runs on Supabase (Supabase, Inc., United States), and the data is stored in a data center in Tokyo. Supabase is entrusted only with storing this app's data and running the server. For how Supabase handles data, see its privacy policy (https://supabase.com/privacy).

Sign-in uses Apple's service (Sign in with Apple).

To review them, hidden words and nicknames are sent to the operator in one email each morning. The email contains only the word or nickname and a short number that tells the account apart. We use Resend (Resend, Inc., USA) to send this email. For how Resend handles data, see its privacy policy (https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy).

Except when required by law, we do not give what we keep to any other person or company.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from "Delete account" in Settings. When you do, everything kept on the server is deleted, and so is everything on your device. You also disappear from other people's Footprints. The link with Sign in with Apple is removed as well.
